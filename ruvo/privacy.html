RUVO - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

RUVO ("the app", "we") is a free collection of mini-games for Android published by Trisoftic. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have. Contact: trisoftic@gmail.com.

1. Who can use what

When you first open RUVO it asks for your age group. This only decides which features are switched on.

  • Under 13: offline play and play on the same Wi-Fi only. No accounts, friends or internet rooms, and only child-directed, non-personalised ads. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents can contact us at the address above.
  • 13 and over: optional online account, friends, internet rooms and standard ads.

2. Stored on your device

Your player name, avatar choices, coins, level, achievements, game statistics, settings and notification history are stored on your device. "Delete my data" in the app removes them from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes them.

3. Online account (optional, 13 and over)

If you create an account or sign in, our server (hosted on Cloudflare) stores:

For email sign-up we send a verification code, and for password reset a reset code, only to the address you enter, through our email provider Brevo. Other players can see your username, avatar and level, never your email address.

4. Internet rooms

Internet rooms use Unity Relay and Unity Authentication (Unity Technologies) to connect players. Unity processes network addresses and an anonymous player ID to deliver game traffic. Same-Wi-Fi play connects devices directly. Game moves and your player name are shared with the other players in the room. There is no text or voice chat.

5. Advertising

RUVO shows ads from Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads). For players under 13 ads are child-directed and not personalised. For others, where required, a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) is shown first and you can change your choice in Settings under Privacy. Google may process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads: see Google's privacy policy.

6. Permissions

Internet and network state (ads, accounts, internet rooms), and vibration (optional). RUVO does not read your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep account data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all linked data inside the app (Profile → Settings → Help & Support → Delete my data) or by following the steps on our data deletion page. Deleting removes the account, sign-in identifiers, friends and block records from our server.

8. Security

Connections to our server use HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect.

9. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
