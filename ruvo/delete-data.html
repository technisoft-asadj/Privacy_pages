RUVO - Delete Your Data

Last updated: 16 October 2026

RUVO is a mini-game app for Android published by Trisoftic. If you created a RUVO account (email, Google, Amazon or guest), you can delete your account and all the data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (instant)

  1. Open RUVO and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile → the gear icon (Settings) → Help & Support.
  3. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

This permanently removes your online account, username, avatar, level and coins, sign-in identifiers (email address and password hash, or Google/Amazon account identifier), friends, block and report records from our server, and the data stored on your device.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can no longer open the app, send an email to trisoftic@gmail.com with the subject "RUVO data deletion", the email address you signed up with (or your RUVO username), and for Google or Amazon accounts the email of that account. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Removing the Google or Amazon connection

You can also remove RUVO's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or from your Amazon account at amazon.com/ap/adam under Login with Amazon.

Data stored only on your phone (settings, offline progress) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. See the full Privacy Policy.
