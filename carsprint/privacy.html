Overview

CarSprint is a free, ad-supported endless driving game published by Trisoftic. Like most free mobile games, it uses ads to stay free and basic analytics to understand how the game is used. This policy covers everything the app and its bundled third-party SDKs are capable of collecting — regardless of whether every feature described here is actively turned on in a given build (see the notes in each section).

2

Information we collect

CarSprint itself never asks you to create an account, log in, or type in personal information. The data below comes from the third-party SDKs the app includes, not from anything you enter.

CategoryWhat it isCollected via
Device & ad identifiers Advertising ID, device/network type, approximate (IP-based) location, ad interaction events Google AdMob (banner, interstitial & rewarded ads)
Usage & performance data Screens viewed, session length, app crashes/errors, general gameplay events (e.g. game started, game over) Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics
Remote configuration No personal data — just which app config values (difficulty tuning, feature flags) your device has fetched Firebase Remote Config
3

Stored only on your device

Your gameplay progress lives entirely on your device via Android's local app storage — it is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Never transmitted anywhere: high score & your local top-5 leaderboard, total coins collected, your selected car, sound/music/vibration settings, and your randomly-generated in-game display name.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's App Info settings, permanently deletes this data.

4

How we use information

  • To show ads (including, if you haven't opted out, ads relevant to your interests) and measure ad performance
  • To understand which parts of the game people actually play, so we can fix bugs and improve it
  • To diagnose crashes and technical problems
  • To adjust difficulty and feature availability remotely without needing a new app update

We do not sell your information, and we don't build advertising profiles beyond what Google's AdMob/Firebase SDKs do on our behalf under their own policies (linked below).

6

Your choices

  • Opt out of personalized ads: Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • Reset your advertising ID from the same screen at any time
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored game data immediately
  • Limit ad tracking further via your device's general privacy settings, which AdMob respects
7

Children's privacy

CarSprint is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information through the app, contact us below and we'll address it.

8

Data retention & security

Data collected by AdMob/Firebase is retained per Google's own retention policies (see their links above), not a schedule we set. Locally stored data (scores, settings) stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. We rely on Google's infrastructure security for any data those SDKs process — CarSprint itself doesn't operate its own backend servers.
