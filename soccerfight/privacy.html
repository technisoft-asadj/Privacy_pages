Soccer Fight

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

Soccer Fight (the "game") is a mobile football game published by Trisoftic ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and how. If you have any question, contact us at trisoftic@gmail.com.

Information we do not collect

The game does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We do not run our own servers that receive data from the game in this version.

Information stored on your device

Your player name, level, coins, settings and match options are saved only on your device so that your progress is kept between sessions. This data never leaves your device through the game. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the main menu, a full-screen ad after some matches, and an optional rewarded video that you choose to watch. To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, to serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud. We do not receive this information.

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's consent form. To change your choice, clear the game's data in Android settings; the form is then shown again at the next start where it is required.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Security and retention

Because the game keeps your progress on your own device and sends nothing to us, we hold no personal data about you. Data handled by Google for advertising is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Your choices and rights

You can clear the game's data from Android settings at any time, withdraw ad consent (by clearing the game's data), and reset your advertising ID. Depending on where you live you may have rights over personal data held by Google; you can exercise them with Google directly.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game works, for example by adding online play, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
