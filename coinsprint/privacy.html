1Overview

CoinSprint ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile endless-runner game published by Trisoftic Games. This policy explains what information the app collects when you play, how it's used, and the choices you have. CoinSprint has no login, no account creation, and no way for us to identify you by name, email, or any other personal detail.

2Information we collect

CoinSprint uses a small number of third-party services to show ads and to help us find and fix crashes. Through those services, the following information may be automatically collected:

CategoryExamplesCollected via
Device & advertising identifiers Advertising ID, device model, OS version, language, general (non-precise) region Google AdMob
App usage & performance Screens viewed, session length, feature usage, app crashes and error logs Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics
Remote configuration Anonymous app-version/config fetches used to tune gameplay difficulty Firebase Remote Config

None of these services receive your name, email address, phone number, or any other directly identifying information from CoinSprint — we don't collect it in the first place.

3Information stored only on your device

To let you play without an account, CoinSprint stores some data locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party:

Stays on your device

Your high score and your best 5 run scores (a local leaderboard), coin balance, selected character and unlocked characters, selected map theme, sound/music/vibration preferences, and an auto-generated anonymous display name (e.g. "Player4821") used only for local display.

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

4How we use information

  • Showing ads — banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads (e.g. "watch an ad to continue your run"), served and personalized by Google AdMob.
  • Keeping the app working — crash and error reports help us identify and fix bugs.
  • Understanding usage — aggregate, anonymous analytics (e.g. which screens are used) help us improve the game.
  • Tuning gameplay — remote configuration lets us adjust difficulty/balance without requiring an app update.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use any of the above to build a profile of you outside of what Google's services do on our behalf (see below).

5Third-party services

CoinSprint uses the following Google services, each governed by Google's own privacy policy:

Google AdMob

Serves and personalizes in-app ads. See Google's Ads Privacy & Terms.

Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config)

Provides usage analytics, crash reporting, and remote configuration. See Firebase's Privacy & Security policy.

6Your choices

  • Ad personalization — you can opt out of personalized ads and reset your advertising ID from your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Uninstalling — removes all locally stored app data from your device.
  • Analytics opt-out — device-level ad tracking limits (above) also reduce data shared with our analytics tools.

7Children's privacy

CoinSprint is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information through the app, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

8Data retention & security

Locally stored data (Section 3) remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Data processed by AdMob and Firebase is retained according to Google's own retention policies. We rely on Google's industry-standard security practices for the services we integrate; no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
