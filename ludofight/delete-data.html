Ludo Fight - Delete Your Data

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Ludo Fight is a Ludo board game for Android published by Trisoftic. If you signed in with Google, a guest account or a username, you can delete your online account and all the data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: Delete inside the game (instant)

  1. Open Ludo Fight and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Online Account and confirm.

This permanently removes your online account, profile name and picture, statistics, rank, friends list and leaderboard entries.

Option 2: Ask us by e-mail

Send an e-mail to trisoftic@gmail.com with the subject "Ludo Fight data deletion" and the name shown on your Ludo Fight profile. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

Removing the Google connection

You can also remove Ludo Fight's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Sign in with Google), choose Ludo Fight and remove access.

Data stored only on your phone (game settings, offline progress) is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. See the full Privacy Policy.
