Little Learners 3D (Kids Learn) - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Little Learners 3D, also known as Kids Learn ("the game", "we") is an educational game for young children (ages 2 to 6) published by Devorbit for Android and Amazon Fire tablets. This policy explains what the game does with information. Contact: trisoftic@gmail.com.

1. Made for children

Kids Learn is designed for children and their parents. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no social features, no user-generated content and no online profiles. We do not ask a child for a name, e-mail address, photo, voice or location, and the game does not use the camera or microphone.

2. Information stored on the device

The game works fully offline. Your progress (stars, stickers, achievements, finished lessons and mini-games) and your settings (music, voice, sound effects, volume, difficulty) are saved only on the device. We cannot see them. They are removed when you uninstall the game, clear its data, or use Reset progress in the Parent Area.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information from children, and the game does not send your progress or settings to us.

4. Advertising

The game is free. The Google Play version shows ads from Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). The Amazon Fire version shows no ads.

  • Child-directed ads only. The game tells Google that it is directed at children, that its users are under the age of consent, and that only ads rated for all audiences (content rating G) may be shown. Ads are not personalised and there is no behavioural targeting. The game does not request or use the device's advertising ID.
  • Where ads appear. A small banner at the bottom of the Games menus; a full-screen ad at a calm break (after a finished mini-game or story, or when leaving a learning screen after some time spent there), shown at most every few minutes and never in the first minutes of play. Ads never appear while a child is answering, playing a game, or listening to a poem or story, and never on the Home screen, the Learning World map or in the Parent Area.
  • What the ad provider may process. To deliver and measure an ad, Google may process technical information such as the device's IP address, device type, operating system and app version. This is handled as child-directed treatment under Google's policies for children's apps. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's guidance on apps for children.
  • Tapping an ad can open a web page or a store page outside the game. Parents may wish to supervise play.

5. Permissions

The game asks only for Internet access and network state, which are used to load ads. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, phone or advertising-ID permissions. The game itself works fully offline; without a connection no ads are shown.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell or share personal information, and we hold none. The only third-party SDK in the game is the Google Mobile Ads SDK described above. The game contains no analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs.

7. Parents' choices

A parent can see progress and change settings in the Parent Area, and can delete all saved progress at any time with Reset progress. Because we hold no data about children, there is nothing to delete on our side. For any question, or if you believe a child has given us personal information, e-mail trisoftic@gmail.com and we will respond and delete anything we find.

8. Third-party content

The game uses openly licensed art, fonts and tools. Credits are shown in Parents > About > Credits.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected in this page and in the store listing.
