Kids Learn - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Kids Learn ("the game", "we") is an educational game for young children (ages 2 to 6) published by Devorbit for Android and Amazon Fire tablets. This policy explains what the game does with information. Contact: trisoftic@gmail.com.

1. Made for children

Kids Learn is designed for children and their parents. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no social features, no user-generated content and no online profiles. We do not ask a child for a name, e-mail address, photo, voice or location, and the game does not use the camera or microphone.

2. Information stored on the device

The game works fully offline. Your progress (stars, stickers, achievements, finished lessons and mini-games) and your settings (music, voice, sound effects, volume, difficulty) are saved only on the device. We cannot see them. They are removed when you uninstall the game, clear its data, or use Reset progress in the Parent Area.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information from children, and the game does not send your progress or settings to us.

4. Advertising

The game is free. Where a release shows ads, they are limited to family-friendly content, are not personalised, do not use behavioural targeting, and are shown only at calm breaks (never while a child is answering, and never during poems or stories). Ad providers may process technical information such as the device's IP address and device type to deliver and measure the ad; this is done as child-directed treatment under the provider's family policies. Some releases may contain no ads at all. The Parent Area is protected by a parent gate; ads never appear in it.

5. Permissions

The game needs internet access only for advertising, if present. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or phone permissions.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information. The game contains no analytics or crash-reporting service that collects personal data.

7. Parents' choices

A parent can see progress and change settings in the Parent Area, and can delete all saved progress at any time with Reset progress. Because we hold no data about children, there is nothing to delete on our side. For any question, or if you believe a child has given us personal information, e-mail trisoftic@gmail.com and we will respond and delete anything we find.

8. Third-party content

The game uses openly licensed art, fonts and tools. Credits are shown in Parents > About > Credits.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
