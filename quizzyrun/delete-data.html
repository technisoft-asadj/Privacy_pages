QuizzyRun - Delete Your Data

Last updated: 23 October 2026

QuizzyRun is a game for Android published by Trisoftic. If you created a QuizzyRun account (email, Google, Facebook or Amazon), you can delete it and everything linked to it at any time.

Option 1: Delete inside the game (instant)

  1. Open QuizzyRun and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account, then Delete forever.

This permanently removes your account, your sign-in link to Google, Facebook or Amazon (or your email address and password hash), your display name, profile-picture link and all of your scores from our server.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can no longer open the game, send an email to trisoftic@gmail.com with the subject "QuizzyRun data deletion", the email address you signed up with (or your display name and the service you signed in with). We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Removing the Google, Facebook or Amazon connection

You can also remove QuizzyRun's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites, or your Amazon account under Login with Amazon. Doing this alone does not remove our copy, so please also use Option 1 or 2.

Guests

If you only played as a guest, we hold no data about you. Everything the game saved stays on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

See the full Privacy Policy. Contact: trisoftic@gmail.com.
