QuizzyRun - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

QuizzyRun ("the game", "we") is a free running and quiz game for Android, for players of all ages including children, published by Trisoftic. This policy explains what the game keeps and why. We keep it as small as we can. Contact: trisoftic@gmail.com.

1. Playing as a guest (the only option for players under 13)

  • No account, name, email address or photo is sent to us. The hero name, coins, scores and settings are saved only on the device, and we cannot see them. They are removed when the game is uninstalled or its data is cleared.
  • Players who say they are under 13 are never offered sign-in, and never play online with other people.
  • The game does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

2. Accounts (optional, players aged 13 and over only)

A player aged 13 or over may sign in with email, Google, Facebook or Amazon. When they do, we store on our server:

  • Email sign-up: the email address, a hashed (scrambled) form of the password, and the display name.
  • Google, Facebook or Amazon sign-in: an identifier from that service, the display name and the link to the profile picture. We do not store the email address from these services and we never see the password.
  • Game data: the player's best scores, so a leaderboard can show the display name, score and profile picture to other players. The display name can be changed by the player at any time.

We use this only to provide sign-in, the leaderboard and the game itself. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising.

How we check age. The game asks for a month and year of birth at first start. This is kept on the device only and decides whether sign-in is offered. We do not send it to our server.

3. Emails

If an email account is created we send a welcome message. If a player asks to reset a password we send a short one-time code. These messages are sent through Brevo. We send no marketing email.

4. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). Ads are requested as child-directed and not personalised, so they are not based on a player's interests. To deliver and measure an ad, Google may process technical information such as the IP address, device type, operating system and app version. See Google's Privacy Policy. Tapping an ad can open a web page or store page outside the game, so parents may wish to supervise play.

5. Where data is kept and for how long

Account data is kept on Cloudflare servers for as long as the account exists. When an account is deleted, the account and all its scores are removed right away (see how to delete your data).

6. Permissions

The game asks only for Internet access and network state, used for ads and for accounts. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or phone permissions.

7. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal information. The services involved are: Google AdMob (ads), Cloudflare (hosting the account server), Brevo (account emails) and, only if the player chooses them, Google, Facebook or Amazon sign-in.

8. Choices for players and parents

  • A signed-in player can delete the account at any time in the game: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • A parent can ask what we hold, or ask us to delete it, by writing to trisoftic@gmail.com.
  • If we learn that we hold personal information from a child under 13 without a parent's permission, we delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
