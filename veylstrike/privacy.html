Veylstrike: Tactical Sniper - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Veylstrike: Tactical Sniper ("the game", "we") is a single-player military shooter for Android published by Trisoftic. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what choices you have. Contact: trisoftic@gmail.com.

1. Who can play

The game contains stylised weapon violence and is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us.

2. Playing offline

The game is fully playable offline and has no accounts, no online multiplayer and no chat. Your mission progress, unlocked weapons, rewards and settings (sound, graphics, sensitivity) are stored only on your device. We do not receive this data.

3. Advertising

Some versions of the game show ads from Google AdMob, for example a banner in a menu screen. Ads are not shown during a mission. AdMob may use your Android advertising ID, device information and approximate location derived from your IP address to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example the EEA and UK) the game asks for your consent first using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later in the game settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Some builds of the game (for example on certain Amazon devices) contain no ads and send no advertising data. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location. It uses the internet only to load ads, where ads are enabled.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your information. The only provider that may process information for us is Google (AdMob), as described above.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings (Settings > Apps > the game > Storage > Clear data). Because we do not hold an account or a copy of your game data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. For questions about advertising data, contact us at trisoftic@gmail.com or Google.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. Contact us at trisoftic@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Security

Network traffic between the game and ad services is encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
