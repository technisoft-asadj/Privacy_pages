Chess Fight - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Chess Fight ("the game", "we") is a chess game for Android published by Devorbit. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what choices you have. Contact: trisoftic@gmail.com.

1. Who can play

Chess Fight is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the online features, contact us and we will delete the data.

2. Playing offline

Offline modes (local two-player, against the computer, learning lessons and puzzles) work without an account. Your player name, settings (sound, music) and local progress (XP, level, chess rating, win/loss record) are stored only on your device.

3. Online play (optional)

Online features (ranked and casual online matches, leaderboards, friends, text and voice chat) are optional. When you use them, the game creates an online player account with Unity Gaming Services (a guest account, a Google sign-in, or a Login with Amazon sign-in on the Amazon Appstore build) and processes:

InformationWhy
An anonymous player ID created by Unity AuthenticationTo identify you in matches and friend lists
Your display name and, only if you choose one, your profile picture (from your Google or Amazon account, or picked on your phone)To show you to the other player in your match and to your friends
Match membership, moves and game resultsTo run the match between phones and to record the outcome
Your online statistics and progress: chess rating, level, XP, wins, losses, drawsFor ranked matches, leaderboards and your profile. Other players can see them (Unity Cloud Save and Unity Leaderboards)
Friend relationships, friend requests, online statusTo provide the friends list and invitations
Your device's IP address (technical)Needed by the connection service to route network traffic

Google sign-in and Login with Amazon let you keep the same online account on several devices. If you use one of them, we receive only the token that proves the login (and, if you allow it, your profile picture and name); the game does not read your contacts, e-mail or other data from your Google or Amazon account. The game does not offer Facebook login.

4. Voice and text chat (optional)

Voice chat is off until you tap the microphone button; only then does Android ask for permission to use the microphone. While voice is on, your voice is transmitted in real time to the other player in your match through Unity Vivox. We do not record or store voice. Text chat messages are sent for the duration of the match only. You can mute your microphone at any time, mute or block the other player, or report them.

5. Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Play build) or Amazon Ads (Amazon Appstore build): an occasional full-screen ad, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch to earn bonus XP; never during a game. These providers may use your device's advertising ID, device information and approximate location derived from your IP address to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example the EEA and UK) the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Privacy Settings. Ad content is limited to a family-friendly rating. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and Amazon's policy: amazon.com privacy notice.

6. Safety and moderation

In online matches you can mute, block or report another player. Reports are sent to us by e-mail together with the match details and the player's ID so that we can review them. We may restrict accounts that break the rules (harassment, hate speech, cheating, illegal content).

7. Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is processed for us by these providers: Unity Technologies (Authentication, Lobby, Relay, Leaderboards, Vivox) - unity.com/legal/privacy-policy; Google (AdMob, and Google sign-in if used); Amazon (Amazon Ads, and Login with Amazon if used, on the Amazon Appstore build). Other players in your match see your display name, picture, and voice/chat messages you send them.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Online account data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your online account and its data at any time inside the game (Settings > Delete online account), or ask us by e-mail at trisoftic@gmail.com; we will delete it within 30 days.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. Contact us at trisoftic@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

10. Security

Network traffic between the game and Unity's services is encrypted (TLS/DTLS). No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will also be announced in the game.
